# Renewal (Recertification) Processing

## When renewal is due

A household's SNAP eligibility is certified for a fixed period, after
which the case must be renewed to continue receiving benefits. Renewal is
not automatic -- a household that does not complete the renewal process
before its certification period ends loses benefits at the end of that
period, regardless of whether its underlying circumstances actually
changed.

## Reviewing prior facts at renewal

Renewal begins by reviewing the household's current on-file facts
(income, composition, shelter cost, resources) rather than starting
intake from a blank case. Confirm which facts are still current and which
need a new effective-dated record before assuming the case can be
re-determined against what's already on file -- a fact that hasn't been
updated since the household's last reported change may simply be
correct, but it must be confirmed, not assumed.

## Verification at renewal

Renewal generally requires re-verifying income even if it hasn't
changed, since the household's most recent income verification may
predate the current certification period. Identity and residency do not
need re-verification at every renewal unless something about the
household's circumstances specifically calls that into question --
re-verifying facts that have no reason to have changed only delays a
renewal without adding useful evidence.

## Expedited screening does not apply at renewal

The 7-day expedited standard is an intake-time screen for a new
application in urgent need; a renewal is evaluated against the standard
30-day processing window regardless of the household's current income
level. A household that would have qualified for expedited service if
this were a new application is still held to the standard renewal
timeline.

## Renewal outcome

A renewal produces a new determination the same way a new application or
a reported-change re-determination does -- it is not a modification of
the prior certification period's own determination. If the household no
longer qualifies, the renewal determination itself is the record of that
outcome, and the AI-drafted notice explaining it follows the same
review-before-approval discipline as any other notice.
